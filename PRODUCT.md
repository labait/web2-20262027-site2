# Molinari

## Register

brand

## Users
Local families looking for fresh seasonal fruit and vegetables, fair prices and a personal relationship with their greengrocer. Most visits should work comfortably from a phone.

## Product Purpose
An Italian landing page for Molinari. Introduce the shop, its direct distributor relationships, small carefully selected supplies and year-round seasonal assortment. Let customers select products and send their basket through WhatsApp. The owner confirmed this audience and ordering flow. No telephone number or address has been provided; these must not be invented.

## Brand Personality
Fresh, genuine and friendly. The owner confirmed an approachable neighborhood-shop identity rather than a supermarket aesthetic.

## Anti-references
Avoid supermarket promotional clutter, anonymous corporate language, intrusive popups and an impersonal mass-market storefront.

## Design Principles
1. Make the business name and the human relationship immediately visible.
2. Let real produce demonstrate freshness and quality.
3. Make selecting a small order straightforward.
4. Be honest about sample prices and missing contact information.
5. Emphasize seasonal variety, careful sourcing and fair value.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
The owner confirmed readable text, sufficient contrast and reduced-motion support. Use semantic HTML, visible focus, accessible control labels and responsive layouts.
